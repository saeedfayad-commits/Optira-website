Dentist

Diagnose and treat oral health conditions, from routine check-ups to fillings, extractions and cosmetic procedures.

A day in the life

People-facing Mixed Solo / downtime
  • 8:00 AM
    Open up & review the day's charts
    Check the appointment list, patient histories and X-rays before the first patient arrives.
  • 8:30 AM
    Morning patient block
    Back-to-back check-ups, cleans and fillings — most of the morning is chairside with patients.
  • 11:00 AM
    Case notes & lab liaison
    Write up notes between patients and call the dental lab about a crown fitting.
  • 12:30 PM
    Lunch break
    Away from the chair — a proper reset before the afternoon list.
  • 1:30 PM
    Afternoon patient block
    More complex procedures — extractions and root canals — needing steady focus with each patient.
  • 3:30 PM
    Treatment planning huddle
    Run through tomorrow's tricky cases with the dental assistant and hygienist.
  • 5:00 PM
    Admin & close out
    Billing, supply orders, and sterilising the surgery ready for tomorrow.

How to get there (NSW pathway)

1
HSC
Biology, Chemistry and Mathematics
2
Study
Bachelor of Dental Surgery or Doctor of Dental Medicine (5 years, or a 4-year postgraduate program)
3
Get qualified
Registration with the Dental Board of Australia via AHPRA

Skills that matter most

Manual dexterity Attention to detail Communication Business sense
